Privacy Policy

Moonmoss Uploader · Effective October 3, 2026

Moonmoss Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the Moonmoss Sanctuary YouTube channel. Its only purpose is to upload videos to that channel. This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Your data is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • Permission to upload and manage videos on the authorized YouTube channel (the youtube.upload and related scopes).
  • Basic channel information needed to confirm which channel videos are uploaded to.
  • An OAuth access token and refresh token issued by Google.

How the data is used

The app uses this access only to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish schedules, and to check upload status on the owner's own channel. It does not read, collect or analyze viewers' personal information.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored securely and used only by the app. No user data is sold, shared with, or disclosed to third parties. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies

The app does not place cookies or tracking technologies on any user's device.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Once revoked, stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while access is authorized. To request deletion of any stored data, email the address below; it will be deleted within 7 days.
