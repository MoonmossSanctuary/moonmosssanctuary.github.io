Moonmoss Sanctuary

Ambient & fantasy sleep music — dragons, enchanted forests, moonlit rivers.

Moonmoss Sanctuary is a YouTube channel publishing long-form ambient soundscapes for sleep, rest and focus.

About the Moonmoss Uploader app

Moonmoss Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Moonmoss Sanctuary channel. It uploads finished videos — with their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing schedules — to that one channel through the official YouTube Data API.

It does not access any other person's account or data, and it is not offered to the public.
